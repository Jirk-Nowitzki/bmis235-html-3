National Parks Road Trip

This site is my plan for a 21-day road trip through the West. We start in Washington, drive down through California, cut across Nevada, Utah and Arizona, head over to Colorado, then go up through Idaho and Montana and finish back in Washington. That is about 5,400 miles and 13 stops.

Route: a map of the whole trip and how far each drive is. Parks: the parks we will visit and what we want to do at each one. Expenses: how much the trip should cost.
