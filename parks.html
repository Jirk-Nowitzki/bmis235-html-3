Things to know

Idaho: Idaho does not have a national park, so Craters of the Moon (a national monument) is our Idaho stop.

Oregon: We have to drive through Oregon to get to California, so I picked Crater Lake as a stop on the way.

Timing: We are going in summer because some park roads, like the Going-to-the-Sun Road, close when it snows. A few parks also ask for timed-entry or vehicle reservations in busy months, so I will check each park's page on nps.gov before we leave.

Entrance fees: One America the Beautiful annual pass covers all of these parks. See the Expenses page for the price.
